# P4-02 action/resource registry and policy decision contract

## Requirement and ownership

P4-02 delivers A402 (the complete registry) and A404 (the decision contract and its
conformance fixtures) under ADR 0008. It blocks P4-03 (capability grants) and P4-05
(enforcing every registered action).

## Delivered

- **Registry** (`contracts/security/actions.json`): 121 actions, 33 resource types, and
  14 conditions. It covers all 224 inventoried surfaces, and the coverage check is in
  the gate (`scripts/generate_security_registry.py`). It also records:
  - each action's legacy guard, next to its least-privilege rule;
  - removals for surfaces that are going away (RL-012 cluster, RL-019 email login,
    P4-04 raw keys, P7-05 legacy sessions);
  - actions for the P4-01 identity lifecycle and for workloads.
- **Domain** (`aseman-domain::authority`):
  - the typed registry and conditions;
  - requests with caller-established facts;
  - decisions with reason codes and explanations;
  - the pure reference evaluator, which denies by default. `granted` holds only for A403
    grants.
- **Contracts** (`aseman-contracts::security`): loads the compiled-in registry. An
  unknown name fails the whole registry.
- **Port:** `PolicyDecisionPort::decide(&PolicyRequest)` replaces the string-based
  `authorize`. `SetDesiredWorkloadState` authorizes a typed subject and establishes the
  `owner` fact for the workload's own creature.
- **Conformance** (`tests/contracts/policy`): 24 normative hand-written cases, with
  `check_provider` for any engine. The reference provider `aseman-policy-native` passes.

## Findings recorded in the registry

- `/creatures/mint` is `user_guard` plus a hard-coded `1@global` check. In the target
  it is a `node_admin` action.
- The gateway topic actions and `/creatures/create` are public in legacy. The registry
  keeps them public, and grants can later narrow them per topic.
- Guest host calls have no authentication (LD-14, LD-24). Their actions require
  `same_creature` or `owner`, which only the P4-04 gateway can establish, so they cannot
  be authorized until then.

## Next

P4-03 covers capability grants: issue, delegate, attenuate by intersection, expire,
explain the parent chain, and revoke descendants (A403). That is what makes `granted`
hold.
